VolleyVision

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

VolleyVision is a volleyball stats app for teams: coaches record matches, and players and staff see stats and talk in team chat. It's run by Himex Trading Ltd, a New Zealand company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Questions or requests: support@volleyvision.co.nz.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to run your teams, nothing for advertising. We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. Your stats and chat are visible only to your own teams.

Who can use VolleyVision

You need to be 13 or older to create an account; you confirm this when you sign up. We don't ask for a date of birth at sign-up.

Players can be younger than 13, or not have an account at all: a coach can add them to a team roster. A player record holds only a name, jersey number, position and match stats. The coach who adds a player is responsible for having the player's permission, or a parent's or guardian's for a child. A parent or player can ask the coach, or us, to correct or remove a record.

On your device

The app keeps your sign-in, your name, recent match rosters and any match taps waiting to sync on your device so it works offline. In the Android and iPhone apps this is the phone's own app storage; on the website it's your browser's storage. We don't use cookies for this. Signing out removes your sign-in and cached details; taps still waiting stay until they sync. Deleting your account removes everything.

Service providers, and where data is stored

We use these companies to run VolleyVision. They act on our behalf, only for these purposes, and must protect your information to a standard comparable to the NZ Privacy Act:

  • Supabase: our database and file storage, in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1).
  • Netlify: hosts the website and the app's server (United States and a global network).
  • Sentry: error reports, with personal details removed (United States).
  • Google (Gmail): sends our emails: sign-up confirmation, password resets, team invitations, and account-deletion confirmation.
  • Apple and Google Play: distribute the apps.

This means your information is stored and processed outside New Zealand. We don't sell or rent it, and we don't share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages: until they're deleted. Deleting a message erases its text, photos and files, not just hides them. Deleting a team erases its chat.
  • Match stats: stay with the team so its history stays accurate, until the team or the player record is deleted. If you delete your account, stats linked to you stay but your name is replaced with "Former player".
  • Reports: kept for moderation. If the person reported deletes their account, the copy of their message is removed from the report; the reason and time stay. Reports you've made are deleted with your account.
  • Invitations: expire after 7 days, and are removed when the team or the invited account is deleted.
  • The record of changes in a team: kept as team history. If you delete your account, your name, email and account id are removed from it.
  • Anti-abuse counters: about an hour.
  • Error reports: for the period our error-reporting provider keeps them, then deleted.
  • Backups: we keep backup copies of the database for TO CONFIRM (how long backups are kept), so we can recover from a failure. Deleted data disappears from them when they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information: most of it is in the app (Profile). For anything else, email us.
  • Delete your account: in the app under Profile → Account → Delete account, or by email. See Delete your account for what's deleted and what's kept.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account, or email us.
  • Complain: email us first; if you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

We'll respond to requests within 20 working days, as the Privacy Act requires.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Each team's information is visible only to its members, files are stored privately and shared through short-lived links, and resetting your password or deleting your account ends every signed-in session.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects how we use your information, we'll tell you in the app, and you may be asked to agree to updated Terms before carrying on.
