Delete your VolleyVision account

Last updated 1 October 2026

In the app or on the website

  1. Sign in and open Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what happens, enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

It happens straight away, and you'll get an email confirming it.

If you own a team, transfer it to another member or delete it first (on the team's page). The app lists those teams and links to them.

Without the app

Email support@volleyvision.co.nz from the email address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We check the request came from that address, delete the account, and reply to confirm, within 20 working days (usually much sooner).

What's deleted

  • Your account, sign-in and profile details.
  • Every chat message you sent, with its photos and files. Others see "Message from a former member was removed".
  • Your feedback and reports, the people you blocked, and invitations sent to your email.
  • Your email address and account id wherever they appear, including your teams' history of changes.
  • Everything the app kept on your device.

What's kept, and why

  • Match stats recorded for you stay with your teams, so their totals and history stay right. Your name on them is replaced with "Former player".
  • Your teams' history of changes stays, with your name and details removed.
  • Reports about your messages keep the reason and when it was reported, not your message.
  • Backups: copies of the database are kept for 48 hours, then replaced; your data disappears from them when they expire.

A player record without an account

If a coach added you (or your child) to a team without an account, ask the team's coach to remove the record, or email us and tell us the team and the player's name.
